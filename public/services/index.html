Six disciplines

What an engagement covers

Every engagement starts with what you already have — your menus, your logs, your purchasing, your people — and ends with a documented system your team can run without us.

01

HACCP & food safety program design

Hazard analysis, critical control points, monitoring logs, and corrective action — documented so the program survives turnover and an unannounced inspection.

02

ServSafe training & proctoring

Certified ServSafe Manager instruction and exam proctoring delivered on site, plus allergen awareness and sanitation coaching for line-level teams.

03

Sustainable sourcing strategy

Regional and regenerative purchasing programs, supplier standards, and seasonal menu calendars that support local economies without breaking the food budget.

04

Waste reduction & whole utilization

Waste audits, root-to-stem production planning, and circular kitchen systems that cut carbon impact and food cost in the same move.

05

Nutrition-first menu development

Plant-forward, scratch-cooked menu architecture with nutrition targets built into every recipe — flavor first, and reproducible on a real line.

06

Culinary training & keynotes

Hands-on skills development, plant-forward technique labs, and executive keynotes that bring the same thinking to where decisions are made.
