Engagement models

Three ways to work together

Every model is scoped to an outcome and priced after a conversation. There is no checkout here — the right scope depends on what we find.

01

Diagnostic

2–4 weeks

A focused read on where the risk and the opportunity actually sit.

On-site assessment of production, sanitation, and purchasing

Menu and recipe review against nutrition and cost targets

Written findings with prioritized, costed recommendations

Executive debrief with your leadership team

Discuss this scope
02

Program build

90 days

A documented system your team owns, not a consultant’s report.

HACCP or sustainability program written to your operation

Recipe and menu development with nutrition targets built in

ServSafe Manager certification for your key staff

Mock inspection and a measurement plan before handover

Discuss this scope
03

Embedded partner

Ongoing retainer

Standing capacity for operators making change across multiple sites.

Monthly on-site or remote working sessions

Quarterly audit against your documented standards

Seasonal menu development and supplier review

Priority access for keynotes and team training days

Discuss this scope

Not sure which fits? Most partnerships begin with a diagnostic and convert into a program build once the priorities are clear. Tell us the operation and the timeline, and we will recommend a scope.

What every engagement includes

Written standards, trained people, measurable results

Nothing is delivered as advice alone. Every engagement ends with documentation your team can follow, training at the station, and a measurement plan that tells you whether it held.
