About

A chef who speaks the language of science and hospitality

Twenty-five years in professional kitchens, credentialed at the intersection of food safety, sustainability, and nutrition.

The principal

Matthew Thompson, WCMC

Chef Matthew Thompson has spent more than twenty-five years proving that food can be both unapologetically delicious and genuinely healing. His work sits where three disciplines meet: food safety, sustainable food systems, and nutrition.

He is a World Certified Master Chef with an MBA from Johnson & Wales University, a ProChef III certification from the Culinary Institute of America, Certified Executive Chef and Certified Culinary Administrator credentials from the American Culinary Federation, and a Harvard Medical School certificate in Lifestyle Medicine.

MBA · Johnson & Wales World Certified Master Chef ProChef III · CIA CEC · CCA · ACF Harvard Lifestyle Medicine

His philosophy is simple and uncompromising: food as medicine, plant-forward and scratch-cooked, regeneratively sourced, and served with care. As he puts it — it’s all transformation.
